# Microsoft Valuation Model

This project contains my first independently created company valuation model and investment thesis.

I built an integrated three-statement model for Microsoft, forecast its financial performance from FY2026 to FY2030, and valued the company using a discounted cash flow analysis.

## Project contents

The Excel model includes:

- Assumptions
- Income statement
- Balance sheet
- Cash flow statement
- Supporting schedules
- DCF valuation
- Sensitivity analysis

I also produced a four-page investment thesis explaining Microsoft's business, investment case, catalysts, risks and valuation.

## Valuation results

- Implied share price: **$239.07**
- Market price: **$393.35**
- Implied downside: **39.2%**
- WACC: **9.81%**
- Terminal growth rate: **2.50%**
- Valuation date: **28 July 2026**

Although the DCF indicates considerable downside, I assigned Microsoft a HOLD rating because my consolidated forecasts may not fully capture the future growth potential of Azure, Copilot and Microsoft's wider AI operations.
